SILICON MARKETING

System & Workflow Architecture Enhancement Proposal
Document: OMS Proposal v2.0
Target: Family Business Review & Sign-Off
Date: October 7, 2026

1. Executive Summary

This proposal establishes a frictionless 5-department order fulfillment lifecycle tailored specifically for Silicon Marketing's wholesale plywood, MDF, and hardware business. It introduces clear client ownership, multi-address support, pricing controls, actual packed-quantity tracking, invoice attachment, multi-order trip bunching, and complete financial audit retention.

2. 5-Stage Operational Pipeline

Step 1
Order Creation
Customer Manager, Multi-Address picker, Urgent toggle, Tax & Last Price notes.
Step 2
Order Approval
Dedicated approval queue. Verifies rates & discounts. Sets final price.
Step 3
Warehouse Packing
Packs goods. Enters actual physically loaded quantity to log shortages.
Step 4
Tax & Billing
Matches packed vs billed qty. Inputs bill no., date & attaches invoice PDF.
Step 5
Fleet Logistics
Assigns driver. Bunches multiple orders in 1 trip. Split batch dispatch.

3. Customer & Product Master Enhancements

A. Customer Master (CRM)

Feature / Field Configuration Business Benefit & Operational Value
Customer Manager Dropdown (Sales / Staff) Assigns clear staff ownership. Sales reps know who is responsible for follow-ups and payments.
Multiple Delivery Addresses Unlimited (Address 1, Warehouse, Site) Supports clients with multiple godowns, show units, or site locations with 1-click order picker.
Privilege Tier Bronze (Default) Silver Gold Categorizes clients for pricing discounts, credit limits, and priority dispatch.
Urgent Client Flag Toggle (Priority Client) Flags VIP contractors so new orders automatically default to Urgent status.
Alternate Contact No. 10-Digit Mobile Separates owner/proprietor contact from the on-site supervisor or purchase manager.
GST Number (GSTIN) 15-Digit Alphanumeric Strict validation prevents typos before tax invoices are generated. Must be unique.
Customer Code / ID Auto-Generated + Editable Auto-generates sequential codes (e.g. CUST-1001). Admins can edit to match Busy ledger codes.
Customer Alias Market Nickname Quick search by the local name verbalized in market trade (e.g. Balaji Ply for Sri Balaji Traders).
Master Field Control Admin Dropdown Management Admins can configure and decide exactly which options appear in dropdown menus during customer creation (e.g. Customer Managers, Privilege Tiers, Payment Terms, Address Labels). Prevents clutter.

B. Product Master (Inventory)

Change Implementation Operational Value
Category → Group Name Rebranding in UI & DB Matches standard timber/plywood trade and Busy groups (Plywood Group, MDF Group, Hardware Group).
Anti-Duplication Rules SKU + Compound Check Strictly unique SKU. Prevents adding identical product name twice inside the same Group.

4. Order Creation & Workflow Controls

Stage Key Features & Functionality
Order Creation
/dashboard/orders/create		 • Customer Manager Badge: Automatically shows assigned manager (uneditable).
• Address Picker: Dropdown to pick Address 1, Godown, or custom site address.
• Urgent Order Toggle: Flags order as Urgent with high-visibility glowing row colors.
• Tax Mode Toggle: Inclusive vs Exclusive of GST per item.
• Product Notes: Box below each item for discount or specification instructions.
• Last Price Checkbox: Flags the approval team to apply the client's last billed price.
Approve Orders
New Sidebar Menu		 • Pricing Review: Management reviews item notes and last price requests.
• Confirm Price: Approver confirms final rates and clicks Approve Order to release to warehouse.
Warehouse Packing
/dashboard/packing		 • Packed Quantity Input: Staff inputs actual physical units loaded (e.g. 85 sheets packed vs 100 ordered).
• Discrepancy Logging: Directly flags warehouse shortages for billing.
Billing Department
/dashboard/billing		 • Reconciliation: Billing adjusts Invoiced Qty to match physical Packed Qty.
• Invoice Record: Enters Bill Number, Bill Date, and attaches digital PDF Invoice.
Fleet Logistics
/dashboard/delivery & App		 • Multi-Order Trip Bunching: Assigns multiple neighboring orders to 1 driver in 1 trip.
• Split / Batch Delivery: Deliver big orders in separate partial truckloads with separate bills.
• GPS Navigation SDK: Driver phone tracks road-snapped location continuously.
Security & Audit Policy • Admin-Only Deletion: Standard staff cannot permanently delete any order.
• Cancellation with Reason: Staff can only Cancel orders with mandatory reason logged.
• Permanent Retention: Cancelled orders are never wiped; kept in records for full audit transparency.

5. Agreed Decisions & Next Steps

  • Privilege Tiers: Bronze (Default), Silver, Gold.
  • Customer Code: Auto-generated (e.g. CUST-1001) with Admin manual override capability (matching Busy ledger codes).
  • Group Name: Categories converted to Group Name convention (matching Busy product groups).
  • Master Field Control: Dedicated Admin settings to configure dropdown options during customer creation.
  • Database Migration: Full Prisma schema updated with new models (CustomerAddress, DispatchBatch, ProductGroup).

Frontline Capabilities: From Field Inquiry to Packed Carton

Module 01 Automated Zero-Disruption Busy Sync Agent
30-Min Snapshot Sync

A dedicated Windows background service (SMFlowSyncAgent.exe) runs quietly on your Busy server or main workstation. It takes atomic snapshot reads of your Busy database file (.bds) without applying table locks or slowing down office billing clerks.

Master Catalog Sync: SKUs, descriptions, brand categories, units, and GST tax slabs (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%).
Live Godown Stock: Automatically pushes current on-hand warehouse quantities to sales reps' mobile phones.
Party Ledgers & Terms: Syncs customer billing addresses, GSTIN numbers, outstanding balances, and credit limits.
Module 02 1-Click WhatsApp High-Res Multi-Page A4 Quotations
Built-in PDF Engine

Replace handwritten rate slips and delayed office typing. Field sales executives can draft, price, and deliver high-resolution PDF quotations directly in front of the customer.

  • Automated PDF Typography: Generates crisp multi-page A4 estimates with company header, client GSTIN, HSN codes, discounts, and payment terms.
  • WhatsApp 1-Tap Copy: Simultaneously copies a clean 7-line WhatsApp estimate summary card to the sales rep's clipboard for instant pasting.
  • Customer Pricing Memory: System instantly remembers and populates the customer's negotiated last-sold prices to prevent margin leakage.
  • 1-Tap Conversion: When the customer approves, 1 tap converts the quote into an active warehouse order.
Module 03
Central Order Intake & Approvals
  • Omnichannel Reception: Ingests orders from field reps, phone desks, and customer B2B request portals.
  • Urgent Order Flagging: Red flash badge prioritizes critical rush jobs at the top of warehouse queues.
  • Salesperson Filtering: Real-time "Placed By" filters across all operational views to track sales team throughput.
  • Granular Line Review: Approve, modify, or trim line quantities based on stock availability before releasing to packing.
Module 04
Warehouse Picking & Order Splitting
  • Interactive Picking Queue: Mobile/tablet checklist for pickers with live checkbox verification.
  • Packaging Recording: Log total cartons, bags, wooden boxes, and gross weights before staging.
  • Intelligent Order Splitting: If partial stock is missing, split an order into -A (ready items) and -B (back-ordered items) in 1 click.
  • No Stock Stalls: Customers receive available goods immediately while back-orders remain cleanly tracked.

Module 05 Pre-Billing Master Integrity Gate
Zero Busy Sync Errors

Prevents the #1 cause of accounting chaos: billing unmapped customers or non-standard items. Flowza enforces an automated validation gate before orders can be dispatched.

1-Click Master Linking: If a field rep typed a custom item or newly onboarded client, billing staff can link it directly to the official Busy item or party master in an in-modal search.
Invoice Document Vault: Record official Busy invoice numbers, attach scanned invoice copies or transport e-waybills, and ensure accounting reconciliation before loading.
Module 06
Multi-Order Trip Dispatch Hub
  • Multi-Stop Route Builder: Bundle 10 to 30 customer deliveries into a single structured driver trip manifest.
  • Drag-and-Drop Sequencing: Reorder stops by geographical sequence to optimize driver routes and minimize fuel costs.
  • 3rd-Party Transport Support: Record outstation LR Numbers, Transporter Name, and vehicle registration numbers with 1 tap.
  • Dedicated Driver Mode: Driver mobile screen with direct Google Maps navigation shortcuts and 1-tap customer calling.
Module 07
Live GPS Fleet Map & 4-Digit POD
  • Live Vehicle GPS Map: Real-time tracking of active delivery vehicles with speed, heading, and stationary indicators.
  • Driver Phone Telemetry: Live battery level and GPS signal quality monitoring to prevent tracking blackouts.
  • 4-Digit Delivery PIN Security: Drivers cannot mark an order delivered without entering the customer's unique 4-digit PIN.
  • Digital Signature & Photo POD: Capture on-screen customer signatures and unload photo proof at the jobsite.
Module 08
Hardware Device Whitelisting & Remote Kill
  • Hardware-Locked Access: Restrict app login strictly to authorized physical smartphones, tablets, and office PCs.
  • Hardware Fingerprinting: Auto-identifies phone make & model (Samsung, OnePlus, Pixel, etc.).
  • 6-Digit Pairing Codes: New staff devices generate an approval code verified in seconds by the business owner.
  • Remote Kill Switch: Instantly revoke device access with 1 click if an employee departs or a phone is lost/stolen.
Module 09
Native Android APK & Push Notifications
  • Dedicated Android APK: High-performance mobile build engineered for wholesale environments and low-connectivity godowns.
  • FCM Push Notifications: High-priority alerts for new orders, approvals, and dispatch assignments.
  • Deep-Linking: Tapping an alert immediately opens the specific order, estimate, or delivery stop.
  • Internal Team Chat: Integrated WhatsApp-style messaging between sales, warehouse, billing, and drivers.
Enterprise Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Keep sensitive purchase costs, margins, and proprietary supplier ledgers strictly hidden. Sales reps only see assigned customers and selling rates; warehouse staff only see quantities and packing manifests.
